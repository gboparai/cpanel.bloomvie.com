  • All children must be registered with complete and accurate information.
  • Parents must inform the daycare of any changes in contact or emergency information.
  • Regular attendance is expected. Please notify the center in case of absence.

2. Drop-off and Pick-up

  • Children must be dropped off and picked up within the center's working hours.
  • Only authorized persons may pick up a child. ID verification may be required.
  • Late pick-up will result in additional charges as per the daycare policy.

3. Health and Safety

  • Sick children should not be sent to daycare. Parents will be notified to pick up if a child falls ill during the day.
  • Allergies and medical conditions must be disclosed during enrollment.
  • Emergency medical care will be sought if necessary. Parents will be informed immediately.
